# Wakey 2.0.4 — submission text

## What's New (App Store, 4000 chars max)

- Skip the walking test during setup if you can't walk right now. You can still use the walk game on your alarms later.
- Already up before your alarm rings? Hold the button for 2 seconds to stop it. That day won't count toward your streak.
- Gentle start: the alarm sound now fades in over 30 seconds, so it wakes you without a jolt.
- Bug fixes and stability improvements.

## Notes for App Review (App Review Information → Notes)

Thank you for reviewing Wakey 2.0.4. No account or sign-in is needed.

What changed in this version:
1. Onboarding: during the walk game step there is now a "Skip walking test" button, so the setup can be finished without walking.
2. Ring screen: holding the button for 2 seconds stops the alarm without playing the wake-up game. It is meant for users who are already awake. That day does not count toward the wake streak.
3. Alarm sound: the in-app alarm sound fades in over 30 seconds. Notification sounds play at normal volume because iOS does not allow fading them.
4. Analytics: we now record when the subscription screen is shown and when a purchase or restore completes, to measure our subscription screen. No personal data is collected beyond what the App Privacy section already lists.

How to test:
- Finish onboarding (use "Skip walking test" on the walk step if you prefer), then create an alarm 1–2 minutes ahead.
- When it rings, either play the game (walk a few steps or solve the math problems) or hold the button for 2 seconds to stop it.
- The walk game uses Motion & Fitness only to count steps while an alarm is ringing. If that permission is denied, the app counts steps with the accelerometer instead, so the alarm can always be dismissed.
- Subscriptions can be tested with a sandbox account. "Restore Purchases" is on the subscription screen.
