How it moves

  • Walks to the left in place: each planted foot slides right at a steady speed, like on a treadmill, so the loop never jumps.
  • Heels land first and toes push off last. The standing leg is nearly straight as the other foot passes, and the knees are worked out every frame, so a planted foot stays on the ground.
  • The body dips 2.5 after each landing and rises 3 as the other foot passes. The lean rocks about 1°.
  • Both arms swing against the legs: the far arm 44°, out in front; the near arm 42°, staying on the ring so it never covers the face. Elbows bend on the forward swing and the forearms trail by 100 ms.
  • The bells dip 2° once per landing.
  • The shadow narrows a little when a foot lifts or the body rises.

Using it

  • Play it only while steps are being counted. When they stop, finish the step and rest.
  • Reduce Motion: hold the 625 ms frame, the Good morning stride itself, with neutral eyes and the closed smile.
  • The face runs separately from the walk, on its own 23.4 s clock, so the two never line up.
  • In the app, draw fresh random numbers for the face instead of the seeded pattern; the rules are in walk-cycle.json.
  • No squash, no springs, no endless wobble.
